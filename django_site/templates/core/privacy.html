Example policy · Last updated August 21, 2026

Information we collect

We collect information you submit through quotation, tracking, contact, and newsletter forms, including your name, contact details, project location, budget range, timeline, and project description.

How we use it

We use submitted information to assess inquiries, communicate about potential or active work, prepare estimates and proposals, manage the quotation workflow, maintain business records, and improve our services.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorized team members and professional partners when needed to evaluate or deliver a project. Records are retained only as long as required for business, contractual, or legal purposes.

Your choices

You may request correction or deletion of eligible personal information and may unsubscribe from marketing updates at any time. Project and transaction records may be retained when required by law or legitimate business obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions, email {{ company.email }}.
